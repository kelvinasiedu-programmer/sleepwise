Organize your sleep symptoms

Answer a short set of cards about your sleep. SleepWise groups what you select into topics worth raising with a clinician, shows you why each one appeared, and gives you questions to bring to an appointment.

This is a demonstration built on sample data. It does not diagnose, and it cannot rule anything out. It has not been reviewed by a sleep-medicine clinician. If you are worried about your sleep or your safety, speak to a clinician. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Your answers stay in this browser tab. They are never saved, and the cards never ask for your name or any identifying detail. Closing the tab erases them.

How it works: for each card choose Applies to me, Doesn't apply, or Not sure. On a phone you can swipe right for applies and left for doesn't apply. Arrow keys work too.
