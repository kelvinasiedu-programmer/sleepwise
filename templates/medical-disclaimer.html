Medical disclaimer

Last reviewed: June 2026

SleepWise provides general educational information from public health databases. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for a qualified doctor or pharmacist.

  • Always consult a clinician or pharmacist before starting, stopping, or combining any supplement or medication.
  • Do not use SleepWise for emergencies. If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.
  • A result of "lower concern" does not mean a combination is safe for you specifically. Absence of a warning is not proof of safety.
  • SleepWise covers a limited set of sleep supplements and known interactions and may not reflect your full medical picture.
  • Supplements are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

By using SleepWise you acknowledge that decisions about your health should be made with a licensed professional. See Methodology for how results are generated and where the limits are.
